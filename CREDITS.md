- [WordPress](https://wordpress.org/) and the `@wordpress/*` packages

  shipped in this plugin.
- [WooCommerce](https://woocommerce.com/) integration is optional and
  feature-detected.
- Tooling: [Composer](https://getcomposer.org/), PHPCS/`wp-coding-standards`,
  PHPStan (`szepeviktor/phpstan-wordpress`), PHPUnit, Brain Monkey, `wp-env`,
  `@wordpress/scripts`, [semantic-release](https://semantic-release.gitbook.io/).
